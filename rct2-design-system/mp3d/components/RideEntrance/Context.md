# RideEntrance

**CANONICAL IMPORT — copy exactly:** `import { RideEntrance } from './components/RideEntrance';`

The classic RCT2 ride ENTRANCE / EXIT hut, modelled on the original entrance sprites: **one small, neat, symmetrical booth, built once and dressed twice.** Dark timber corner posts with base blocks and capitals, battened cream wall panels, a framed pass-through doorway in the front (+z) and back (−z) walls under a full-width timber head beam, and a saddle roof whose RIDGE runs along the walk axis so a real GABLE faces the queue. The roof overhang is fully SYMMETRIC (0.13 past the wall line in ±x, 0.08 past the gable walls in ±z) and dressed as a roof rather than a plank: shingle course lips, eave fascias, rake (barge) boards standing proud along both gable edges, and a ridge cap sized to swallow their tips.

Both kinds share ONE marquee frame — a timber shelf on two knee braces carried off the head beam and the front capitals, a scalloped valance hanging under it, and a sign board standing on the shelf between pilasters under a cornice rail. The board is sized to the BUILDING: it tops out at **1.14 (entrance) / 1.09 (exit) against a 1.21 ridge and a 1.25 overall height**, so the silhouette stays hut-shaped and the roof, not the sign, wins it. The ENTRANCE is the dressed sibling: taller board, GREEN band, a stepped centre crest that stops under the ridge, and a row of nine marquee bulbs. The EXIT is the plain sibling: same shell, same roof, same shelf and valance, lower board, RED band, one small bracket lamp on the cornice. Green + crest = entrance, red + plain = exit, at a glance — and they read as a matched pair, which is how they are always seen (a ride places both).

Sign lettering is a recessed LED DOT-MATRIX screen (`mountLedScreen` / `ledScreenTexture` in `index.tsx`): a procedural CanvasTexture — dark bezel with a deterministic hashed-sine speckle behind a full grid of pixel squares, lit squares forming the word via a hand-built 5×7 glyph font (no canvas `fillText`, no font dependency, same "draw the glyphs yourself" spirit as NeonSign's vector font). Applied as both `map` and `emissiveMap` on a thin plane flush with the band face, tucked 1.5 mm under the white frame stripes' proud lip so it reads as inset. Entrance glows green ("ENTRANCE", `0x39ff6a`, 0.70 wide), exit glows red ("EXIT", `0xff3b30`, 0.39 wide) — both sized to the BAND, not to the hut. `emissiveIntensity` is gated by `nightKOf` (smoothstep 0.16 → ~2.26) exactly like the marquee bulbs and the exit lamp, so signs read as printed colour by day and light up after dark.

`buildRideEntrance(t, { kind?: 'entrance' | 'exit' })` → `{ group, doorway }` where `doorway` is the local ground-level walk-through point in front of the hut (`[0, 0, 0.66]`, also exported as `ENTRANCE_DOORWAY`) — the GameManager routes peeps through it (queue → doorway → ride platform, and the reverse through an exit hut). The hut faces local +z; **base slab 1.09 × 0.94** (what `GameManager`'s `hutRect` audits at hx 0.55 / hz 0.50), doorway 0.42 wide × 0.72 clear under the head beam, sized for 0.5-scale peeps (0.561 tall — 0.16 of headroom). No update function: the lamps ride `onBeforeRender` + `nightKOf`.

## WHERE THE PAIR STANDS, AND WHICH WAY IT FACES (2026-07 — the RCT2 entrance/exit fix)

They are always seen as a pair, and RCT2 has an exact arrangement for them. The huts are placed by `GameManager/registry.ts registerRide`, from `queueAnchor`/`queueDir` and `exitPoint`/`exitDir`; this component only supplies the two buildings.

**FACING.** Both huts' `rotation.y` is `atan2(dir.x, dir.z)` for the hut's **OUTWARD** direction — local +z, the doorway side, points AWAY from the ride at the path. That matters because RCT2 stores the opposite: an `EntranceElement`'s `direction` points **INWARD**, at the station (`Ride::validateStations` looks for the station track at `location + CoordsDirectionDelta[direction]`, `RideConstruction.cpp:1557-1559`) and both the queue and the footpath may only attach on the reverse side (`kEntranceDirections` = 4 → relative direction 2 only, `EntranceElement.cpp:22-26` + `Footpath.cpp:118-121`). Our `queueDir`/`exitDir` are that reverse — the same sense as the UI's own `gRideEntranceExitPlaceDirection`, which is documented in-source as "faces away from the station" (`openrct2-ui/ride/Construction.cpp:479-483`). A direction is **one of four cardinals** and nothing else; before this fix `registerRide` derived the exit hut's yaw from the `boardPoint → exitPoint` vector, which put huts at diagonal yaws RCT2 cannot represent (measured: `[-0.47, -0.89]`, `[0.51, 0.86]`, …).

**POSITION.** The pair stands ADJACENT — one tile (1.2 u) apart along the SAME station face, both facing the same way out:

```
entrance hut  =  queueAnchor − queueDir·0.62
exit hut      =  entrance hut ± 1.2·[−queueDir.z, queueDir.x]
exitDir       =  queueDir
```

so the queue runs IN beside the exit and the exit's own ordinary footpath runs OUT beside the queue. At 1.2 the two 1.09-wide slabs clear each other by 0.1 u — the tightest legal RCT2 pitch, and SAT-clean against `hutRect`.

Note that RCT2 **does not enforce this**, and the design system does not either: `RideStation` holds two independent `TileCoordsXYZD` (`Ride.h:172-173`) and no code anywhere compares them — each is validated only against the station track. A long station with the entrance at one end and the exit at the other is authentic RCT2. Adjacency is the DEFAULT and an `exitNotAdjacent` **warning**, never a hard error (rules/park-generation.md §0.4b).

**THE TWO PATHS ARE DIFFERENT KINDS.** The entrance takes the red, RAILED `buildQueueLane` — a queue is only enterable at its open tail. The exit takes `buildExitLane`, an ordinary grey **unfenced** footpath (`SURFACE_TARMAC`), because a leaving guest steps onto it from the doorway rather than filing along it. That is not decoration: RCT2 puts a guest finishing a ride down on the tile immediately OUTSIDE the exit (`Guest.cpp:4412-4452` → `:5092-5140`), drops them onto bare terrain via `PeepState::falling` when no path is there (`Peep.cpp:781-890`), and flags the ride `STR_EXIT_NOT_CONNECTED` — *"has no path leading from its exit! Construct a path from the ride exit"* — for as long as it is open (`Ride.cpp:2076`).

The **green crest / red plain** dressing is what tells the two apart at a glance, and with the pair now standing side by side on one face that reading is doing real work: it is the only thing distinguishing the hut you queue for from the hut you come out of.

Original three.js composition on the shared Stage; proportions and the green/red sign convention referenced from the RCT2 entrance sprites, realistic palette only.

## Audit — 2026-07-25, 100/100

Round-9 REBUILD of the massing. The complaint was shape: the entrance's false front was a stack of four plain boxes climbing to y 1.81 over a 0.78 wall (2.3× the wall height), the LED sign dominated the whole kiosk, the roof panels were trimmed asymmetrically (`roofZ −0.09`, `roofD = D + 0.22`) so from a three-quarter view only their raked side edges showed and they read as two loose diagonal planks, and the exit's roof (`roofD = D + 0.4` over a `D` of 0.8) overhung 50 % of its own depth and read as one skewed slab floating over the posts — a lean-to, not a kiosk.

Fixed this pass:
- **Massing.** Overall height 1.81 → 1.25; sign top 1.68 → 1.14 (1.45 × wall height); the ridge is now the tallest thing on the hut. Entrance and exit are the SAME building — same shell, same roof, same shelf and valance — differing only in the board and its dressing.
- **The roof.** One symmetric roof for both kinds (pitch 0.52, ±0.13 x / ±0.08 z overhang) replacing two asymmetric trimmed ones, and it is now detailed as a roof: course lips, eave fascias, proud rake boards, a ridge cap. Deleted `roofZ`, the per-kind `roofD`/`pitch`, and the ridge-beam `beamD`/`beamZ` tuck.
- **The sign is on the building.** A marquee — shelf + braces + valance + board + pilasters + cornice — standing clear in FRONT of the front rake, so nothing needs trimming or tucking. LED resized to the band (0.8 → 0.70 entrance, 0.52 → 0.39 exit) and the band from 0.86 × 0.30 to 0.82 × 0.185.
- **Gaps closed.** Full-width head beams over both doorways (the old short lintels left an open notch beside each) and pentagon gable infills whose rake edges follow `underAt(x)` exactly (the old triangles left a wedge at each corner).
- **Patches deleted, not inherited.** Six of the eight tuck/z-fight workarounds in the old file are gone with the geometry that needed them: the trimmed front roof, the 12 mm ridge-beam tuck off the board's back face, the two THICKNESS-AWARE false-front step boxes ("its old 1.10 top poked THROUGH the underside"), the "proudest element z ≤ 0.555" bookkeeping, the exit band's "no red sliver peeks over the roof rake" height limit, and the exit finial "moved off the band's underside". The two that survive are ordinary construction offsets, not workarounds: the gable's 30 mm inset behind the wall face, and the LED's 1.5 mm reveal under the frame lip. One new deliberate overlap is documented in code — the cornice rail bites 4 mm down over the board's top face, because coincident faces z-fight and a cornice that merely rests on a board is exactly that.
- **Preview layout.** The two huts were spread along the 45° camera's screen-x diagonal, which put one 2.6 u nearer than the other: the near one was seen from almost straight above (its marquee vanished under its own roof overhang) and the far one from a shallow angle, so the pair could not be compared — the whole point of the preview. They are now side by side in x, and a second preview shoots them from the park camera (~50°) where the MASSING, not the lettering, is what is being graded.
