# ParkBuilder

**CANONICAL IMPORT — copy exactly:** `import { parkComposition, validatePark, laneLenOf, terrainLint, offPathCell } from './components/ParkBuilder';`
*(never from `'./Park'`: only the `<Park>` wrappers + track-piece JSX live there. A wrong

Park COMPOSITION UTILITIES + the `validatePark` ACCEPTANCE GATE. This module does NOT generate parks — the agent designs every park from the building blocks (`rules/park-generation.md` is the recipe book) and must run `validatePark` before a park "opens". The preview (`ParkBuilder.previews.tsx`, `buildExamplePark`) is the compact WORKED EXAMPLE of the whole compose-then-validate workflow: explicit literal calls in recipe order — `parkComposition` → `buildTerrain`/`buildWater`/`tintTerrainForClimate` → `buildParkEntrance` on the flat apron → a lattice street skeleton with a fountain plaza (`buildPathNetwork`, `grid: true`) → a wooden `buildRideSpline` coaster + a spinner podium + a snack kiosk + restroom + torches/planting — everything registered with ONE `createGameManager` (the gate is the sole spawn), clickable rides/guests (RideViewer / GuestInfo / ParkInfo), `validatePark` logged at the end.

Exports:

- `parkComposition(t, seed, size = 48, climate?, guards?)` → RCT2-scenario-style landform, PROBED over deterministic terrain-noise seeds until the composition rules hold — and every seed draws its own LANDSCAPE CHARACTER: a mountain ARCHETYPE (`mountainStyle`: `'alpine'` one dominant 4-6-peak ridge / `'rolling'` 2-4 low broad ranges / `'sentinel'` one steep landmark peak / `'twin'` 2-3 medium ranges, placed on seeded edge/corner slots, drama scaling with the plot), TWO flood-filled water bodies on any plot ≥ `TWO_WATER_MIN_SIZE` = 64 (ONE below it — the classic rule, kept bit-identical for the ≤48 parks): a DOMINANT body whose STYLE varies (`waterStyle`: `'central'` lake / `'corner'` lagoon / winding `'river'` chain / classic NE `'flank'` — candidates probed in seeded preference order, first to fully satisfy the rules under YOUR guards wins), themed terrain SECTIONS (`landZones` `{ kind: 'sand'|'meadow'|'mountain'|'forest', center, radius }` + the `zoneAt(x, z)` classifier + `treeline`/`sandBand`), a sand/palm beach flank, a flat dry front apron. Zone counts SCALE with the plot: size ≥ 32 composes MULTIPLE distinct meadow build fields (real districts), size ≥ 40 adds a bonus hill range (measured 3-7 ranges carrying 7-17 peaks at size 128, summits h 3.1-12.2; 2-5 clusters at 48) + an extra forest disc (+1 more at ≥ 80); compact 16 plots compose bit-identically to the classic tables. Build with `firmShore: true` so noise never pools stray puddles. `guards.keepDry` = world cells YOUR layout will build on (probed dry + sane); `guards.coasterPts` = planned coaster control points `[x, yAboveRef, z]` (peaks pre-capped via the internal `capPeakForCoaster`, footings probed). Returns `{ terrainSeed, climate, waterKind, basins, basinsSecond, waterCentre, waterCentreSecond, waterStyle, waterStyleSecond, peaks, clampPeaks, clampBasins, hillClusters, beachDir, sandSpots, palmSpots, zones, report }` — `basins` holds BOTH bodies' bowls (the secondary's are the tail slice, also published as `basinsSecond`), and `report` gains `waterAreas` (per-body, biggest first) and `waterGap` (between the two biggest) — build the real terrain from `terrainSeed` + `[...peaks, ...clampPeaks]`/`[...basins, ...clampBasins]` + `TERRAIN_BASE` at `WATER_LEVEL`. **Guards are POST-ENFORCED (round-3 safeguard):** when no probed seed fully satisfies the rules, the composition CLAMPS the terrain instead of only warning — blend-radius `clampPeaks` raise every wet guarded cell/coaster footing dry, `clampBasins` shave bulges flat under guarded footprints (killing hoisted-path causeways at the root), and severed ponds the clamp bisected off the ONE water body are filled back above the waterline (largest body wins). `report.violations` reflects the POST-CLAMP terrain (empty = clean; `report.clampedCells` counts the discs) — agents can't read consoles, so the guarantee is in the ground itself. Deterministic. **`size` DEFAULTS TO 48, WHICH IS NOT `<Park size>`'s 128 — ALWAYS PASS IT EXPLICITLY.** `<Park>` is at 128 (2026-07) and this default stays at 48 on purpose (every direct caller passes `size`, and raising it would silently re-compose the §1 seed table); omitting it on a 128 park gives you a 48-plot composition whose water and ranges are well under half the linear scale, land in the wrong place, and compose ONE water body where the park is validated for TWO. See the parameter note in `composition.ts` and `rules/park-generation.md` §7.
- **Round-5 safeguards on the clamp:** a WATER-FRACTION GUARD — clamping can never starve the park's water below the climate ideal (≥ max(3% of the plot, the climate's `minWaterU2`)): wet guarded cells raise on TIGHT steep-bank discs (radius 1.4, not the 2.4 blend) and, when filled-back lobes still leave the body small, `parkComposition` GROWS it back with probed edge basins on its open side (one body, never under guards/coaster/forecourt). And **`reclampTerrain(t, comp, rects, coasterPts?)` → `{ touched, raised, shaved, filled, expanded }`** — the AUTO-keepDry pass: re-runs the guard clamp over RUNTIME-DERIVED state (every audited footprint rect + as-built circuit points; authors' `keepDry` is a hint, not a requirement), appending discs to `comp.clampPeaks/clampBasins` IN PLACE so a terrain rebuild reproduces the healed landform. Wet samples raise to WL+0.3, bulges shave to 0.72, coaster points are ANTI-BURIAL caps (ground ≤ railY − 0.35 — footings standing in water are the RCT2 look and are never "dried"), the forecourt stays flat, one-body + water-fraction re-enforced, and corrections UNDO earlier opposite corrections near the same cell (never a raise/shave tug-of-war). `<Park>`'s settle pass calls it and `<Terrain>` rebuilds mesh/colours/heightAt in place before `validatePark` reads anything. Deterministic.
- Climate layer: `ParkClimate` (`temperate | desert | alpine | coastal`), `CLIMATES`, `climateOf(seed)` (hashed pick), `CLIMATE_SPECS` (water story, beach rays, species mix per zone, zone names, tint), `PARK_THEMES` (stripe/canvas/wood/light palettes), `tintTerrainForClimate(t, terrain.mesh, climate, basins, comp?)` (vertex-palette bias: desert tans, alpine darkens + snow-caps, fade tints keep a real beach ring; pass `comp` — as `<Terrain>` does — to ALSO paint the terrain sections: gold sand, saturated meadow, darker forest floor, banded rock above `comp.treeline`).
- `dressTerrain(t, comp, heightAt, { obstacles?, density?, noDress? })` → `{ group, counts, obstacles }` — deterministic AUTO-DRESSING of the sections: Kit-tree clusters over the forest discs (climate species mix, every 2nd tree `lodDetail`-tagged), RockCluster outcrops + loose scree on the mountain flanks, dune mounds/palms/one flavour prop on the sand flank, sparse meadow loners. Keeps clear of the water, steep rock, the gate forecourt, every `comp.guardCells` (keepDry) cell, the planned coaster envelope and your obstacle discs;
  - **`noDress: NoDressRegion[]` — ground that must stay BARE (discs `{ x, z, r }` and/or `ParkFootRect`-shaped rects `{ cx, cz, hx, hz, yaw? }`, world coords).** The planting-only exclusion, and the RIGHT tool when a land paints its own floor (fresh lava, a salt pan, a ceremonial court): every planter tests its sample point — plus the plant's own radius, so nothing overhangs — against these regions and rejects it. **It is not `keepDry` and must not be replaced by one:** keepDry guarantees dry/flat-ish ground and therefore MOVES the heightfield via the guard clamp, which moves the walk network and can break the acceptance sim (the Emberfall Caldera land's ash field did exactly that: one aesthetic fix, one FATAL `sim` failure — that land macro was removed in v6.0, but the lesson stands). `noDress` changes nothing but which objects get planted; omitted or empty it is bit-identical to before the option existed. Exposed on `<Terrain noDress>`; a hand-built themed court publishes its own bare-ground regions the same way (e.g. a lava world's ash-field disc, or a ceremonial court rect) and passes them through. counts scale with area, well under the area-scaled `2500·(size/16)²` mesh budget (160 000 at the default size 128). `<Terrain>` mounts it automatically; imperative parks add `dressed.group` and feed `dressed.obstacles` to their scenery sampler.
- Placement helpers: `terrainLint(heightAt, size, waterLevel?)` → `{ isDry, slopeAt, flatEnough, inBounds }`; `obbOverlap(a, b)` (the 2D SAT rect test, exported for the register wrappers' round-4 queue-orientation safeguard); `planRideAccess(nodes, tailNode, dir, capacity, exit, exitDir)` + `laneLenOf(capacity)` → a GameManager-compatible `RideAccess` (queue tail ON a street node, hut at the lane head, exit on its own cell); `bermNetToGround(t, g, net, pathY, groundAt)` (earth berms + node footings so slabs never float); `plinthUnder(t, g, groundAt, x, z, w, d, topY)`; `groundRideAccess(t, g, groundAt, acc, y, resolvedExitXZ?)` (hut plinths + lane berm, returns obstacle discs).
- **`validatePark(t, { net, manager, terrain, coasters?, footprints?, group?, rebuild?, simSeconds?, gridNodeCount?, probeViolations?, pathY?, plazas?, pathWidth?, planted?, lints? })`** → `{ ok, failures, warnings, simSeconds }` — accessibility (gate routes to EVERY queue tail; **and since 2026-07 check a4, THE EXIT SIDE TOO** — every ride must have an EXIT PATH (`exitLaneLen > 0`) and that path must route back to the gate, or it is a hard `accessibility` FAIL naming RCT2's own `STR_EXIT_NOT_CONNECTED` (Ride.cpp:2076); entrance and exit not ADJACENT on the same outward face is an `exitNotAdjacent` **warning**, because RCT2 provably does not enforce it — `RideStation` holds two independent `TileCoordsXYZD` (Ride.h:172-173) and no code compares them. AND the gate spawn stands at the terrain edge — tolerance `max(1.25, size×0.025)` u, size-relative — **3.2 u at the default 128**, 1.25 u at 48; a gate buried inside the park FAILS); street grid (with `gridNodeCount` = the RENDERED lattice's node count, any diagonal street edge FAILS — RCT2 paths run N/S/E/W; routing-attach spurs are exempt; `<Park>` passes it automatically); terrain vibe (exactly `waterBodyTarget(size)` water bodies — **TWO** on any plot ≥ 64, a dominant one plus a secondary at ≥ 16% of its area and ≥ `max(5, 0.055·size)` u clear of it; ONE below 64; 3+ is still a scatter FAIL — flat dry apron, no footprint on a hill flank, and — round-2 safeguards — a WET-PAD guard: every audited pad/queue-lane/hut rect must stand on ground above `waterLevel + 0.05` (centre + corners sampled; a Teacups pad planted in the lake used to pass), plus `probeViolations` = `parkComposition`'s `report.violations` — non-empty means NO terrain seed fully satisfied the composition rules and is a hard terrain FAIL instead of the old console-only warn; `<Park>` passes it automatically); footprint OBB SAT sweep PLUS any residual `manager.accessAudit()` placeAccess collision (an access overlap is a FAIL, never a console-only warn); coaster legality (a `compileTrackPieces` `fatal` circuit — pass it via `coasters[].fatal` — is ONE hard failure; otherwise `checkCoasterDesign` + IN-BOUNDS: every sampled track point within ±size/2 + 0.4 + `validateSpline` + crash-free: worst lateral G under 1.5 g × 0.85; the re-check saturates `coasters[].bank` at the type's bankLimit via `coasterBankCap` — same clamp the kit builds with); a GameManager smoke run over `max(SIM_SMOKE_SECONDS, 2.5·maxRideDuration + 20)` sim-s so long rides can complete a cycle (≥1 ride cycle, no stuck walkers, no stalled queue — **`SIM_SMOKE_SECONDS` is 85 since 2026-07-28, raised from 60 with the GameManager's BOARDING-QUIET departure rule, and the stall threshold is now `max(45, round(0.75·secs))` = a fraction of the OBSERVATION WINDOW rather than the old `max(30, maxDur + 15)` guess at one cycle, which measured under the real cycle length and turned into a false FAIL as soon as the window was long enough to observe it — derivations are in the source comments beside both constants**; a registered `rideDuration > 14` draws a soft "acceptance sims need ≤ ~12 s rides" warning); mesh budget — AREA-SCALED, `2500·(size/16)²` (**160 000 at the default 128**, ~22 500 at 48); exceeding it is a `console.warn`, NOT a hard fail (LOD + fog keep big parks drawable, but warnings are fatal by the recipe-book policy) — + double-build determinism hash; and the TRACK-CORRIDOR sweep (round-4, failure kind `corridor`): a 1.6-u-wide corridor of segment OBBs along every registered circuit's sampled polyline must be CLEAR of stall footprints (`manager.stalls()`), OTHER rides' pads/huts/lanes (the ride's OWN access is exempt — pass `coasters[].name`, the registered ride name; `<Coaster>`/`<TrackRide>` do) and street edges at grade — an obstacle only fails when the track passes within 2.2 u of its level (`pathY` = the slab walking level for the street check; default terrain height), so flying OVER on a high leg stays clean while grade crossings and roof-skims FAIL naming the offender. The smoke run advances the sim clock — shift your updater time by the report's `simSeconds` afterwards, as `<Park>` and the example do. **ROUND-6 ADDITIONS.** (1) `lints` — build-time auto-fix events (`<Park>` passes `ParkContext.reportLint` records): every one is echoed in the new `report.warnings: { kind, detail, fatal }[]` and the `fatal` ones become hard `'autofix'` failures, because rules/park-generation.md §0 calls a rescued design fatal — a queueDir auto-flip, a hoisted causeway, a corridor shift or a relocated exit hut used to leave `ok: true`. An EMPTY `warnings` array is the only clean result. (2) `'bounds'` — every audited footprint's SWEPT corners and every GameManager-DERIVED queue-tail/exit attach node (`net.nodes[accessPoints().rides[].queueNode]`) must sit inside ±size/2; a capacity-10 rig against the fence used to put queue tails a metre off the plot and pass. (3) `'scenery'` — `planted` (`{ label, x, z, r }`, from `<Scenery>`/`<Placed>`) is audited for DRYNESS (waterLevel + 0.05 under the footprint ring) and for standing inside a rendered path slab (`pathWidth`, default 1.1; `plazas` are exempt dressing; the blocking radius caps at 0.6 u so a walk-in gazebo abutting a path is legal). (4) the track-corridor sweep gains a STRICT re-audit: beside the 1.6-u corridor / 2.2-u ground clearance, every rect is re-swept 2.2 u wide against the obstacle's OWN ROOF (hut 1.5, stall canopy 2.1, lane 0.45, pad 0.6, + 0.3 clearance) — a relocated hut 0.96 u from the rails with the track 0.59 u off the ground used to pass.

- **`reportPlanLint(kind, detail, fatal?)` / `parkPlanLints()` / `clearParkPlanLints()`** (wave-9 P0-A, `planLints.ts`) — the PLAN-TIME LINT BUS. Set-piece planners and `buildParkNet` run at MODULE SCOPE, before React mounts anything, so they must never `throw`: a throw there takes the whole page and the park cannot even be diagnosed (it cost two generated parks their entire score — black frames, no `validatePark` line). They DEGRADE instead (L-route a diagonal boulevard, fall back to a real port, drop a bad edge, resolve a missing cell to the nearest node) and record the defect here; `<Park>` replays the bus into `validatePark`, where the fatal ones become `'autofix'` failures. Every `buildParkNet` warning is on this bus too, so `NET.warnings` really does refuse the build. `parkPlanLints()` READS (it does not drain) and entries are deduped, so a remounted park reports the same defects once.
- **`SEED_TABLE_128` / `SEED_TABLE_SIZE` / `seedTableRow(seed, climate, size)`** (wave-9 P4; regenerated 2026-07 at 128 with two bodies) — the 16 published §1 SEED TABLE rows as data (measured wet-region centroid + water fraction for BOTH bodies — `waterCentroid`/`waterFrac` and `waterCentroid2`/`waterFrac2` — UNGUARDED, at size 128 only — nothing in that table transfers to another plot size). `<Park>` passes the matching row to `validatePark` as `expectWater` (a LIST of expected bodies, dominant first; measured and expected are paired by nearest centroid, not by rank); the gate flood-fills the BUILT heightfield and warns `waterRePicked` (> 10 u centroid delta, naming which body moved), `waterShrunk` (< 75% of the row's area) or `waterGrew` (> 133% of it — the log-symmetric partner, added wave-10 after a park doubled its lake and passed silently) — the composition re-picks its water candidate whenever a `keepDry` cell lands in the listed wet box, and a park that planned a lakeside district off the row otherwise never finds out. Regenerate the rows with `harness/park-eval/seed-table.mjs`.
- **`validatePark` also gains (wave-9): `expectWater`** (above) and a **`deadStreetNode`** warning — every RENDERED street node of degree ≤ 1 (counting the manager's `routing.attach` spur edges, so anything a queue/exit/stall/gate hangs off is exempt) with no footprint/stall/blocker within 2.4 u. `buildParkNet` prunes unwired PORTS; nothing used to prune a spur node the author wrote by hand, and round 8 shipped a "restroom spur" and an "observation-tower spur" with nothing on them.

Fully deterministic — hashed-sine randomness only; the same seed always composes identically.
