# ParkBuilder

Park COMPOSITION UTILITIES + the `validatePark` ACCEPTANCE GATE. This module does NOT generate parks — the agent designs every park from the building blocks (`rules/park-generation.md` is the recipe book) and must run `validatePark` before a park "opens". The preview (`ParkBuilder.previews.tsx`, `buildExamplePark`) is the compact WORKED EXAMPLE of the whole compose-then-validate workflow: explicit literal calls in recipe order — `parkComposition` → `buildTerrain`/`buildWater`/`tintTerrainForClimate` → `buildParkEntrance` on the flat apron → a lattice street skeleton with a fountain plaza (`buildPathNetwork`, `grid: true`) → a wooden `buildRideSpline` coaster + a spinner podium + a snack kiosk + restroom + torches/planting — everything registered with ONE `createGameManager` (the gate is the sole spawn), clickable rides/guests (RideViewer / GuestInfo / ParkInfo), `validatePark` logged at the end.

Exports:

- `parkComposition(t, seed, size = 48, climate?, guards?)` → RCT2-scenario-style landform, PROBED over deterministic terrain-noise seeds until the composition rules hold — and every seed draws its own LANDSCAPE CHARACTER: a mountain ARCHETYPE (`mountainStyle`: `'alpine'` one dominant 4-6-peak ridge / `'rolling'` 2-4 low broad ranges / `'sentinel'` one steep landmark peak / `'twin'` 2-3 medium ranges, placed on seeded edge/corner slots, drama scaling with the plot), ONE flood-filled water body whose STYLE varies (`waterStyle`: `'central'` lake / `'corner'` lagoon / winding `'river'` chain / classic NE `'flank'` — candidates probed in seeded preference order, first to fully satisfy the rules under YOUR guards wins), themed terrain SECTIONS (`landZones` `{ kind: 'sand'|'meadow'|'mountain'|'forest', center, radius }` + the `zoneAt(x, z)` classifier + `treeline`/`sandBand`), a sand/palm beach flank, a flat dry front apron. Zone counts SCALE with the plot: size ≥ 32 composes MULTIPLE distinct meadow build fields (real districts), size ≥ 40 adds a bonus hill range (2-5 clusters on the default 48) + an extra forest disc (+1 more at ≥ 80); compact 16 plots compose bit-identically to the classic tables. Build with `firmShore: true` so noise never pools stray puddles. `guards.keepDry` = world cells YOUR layout will build on (probed dry + sane); `guards.coasterPts` = planned coaster control points `[x, yAboveRef, z]` (peaks pre-capped via the internal `capPeakForCoaster`, footings probed). Returns `{ terrainSeed, climate, waterKind, basins, peaks, clampPeaks, clampBasins, hillClusters, beachDir, sandSpots, palmSpots, zones, report }` — build the real terrain from `terrainSeed` + `[...peaks, ...clampPeaks]`/`[...basins, ...clampBasins]` + `TERRAIN_BASE` at `WATER_LEVEL`. **Guards are POST-ENFORCED (round-3 safeguard):** when no probed seed fully satisfies the rules, the composition CLAMPS the terrain instead of only warning — blend-radius `clampPeaks` raise every wet guarded cell/coaster footing dry, `clampBasins` shave bulges flat under guarded footprints (killing hoisted-path causeways at the root), and severed ponds the clamp bisected off the ONE water body are filled back above the waterline (largest body wins). `report.violations` reflects the POST-CLAMP terrain (empty = clean; `report.clampedCells` counts the discs) — agents can't read consoles, so the guarantee is in the ground itself. Deterministic.
- **Round-5 safeguards on the clamp:** a WATER-FRACTION GUARD — clamping can never starve the ONE body below the climate ideal (≥ max(3% of the plot, the climate's `minWaterU2`)): wet guarded cells raise on TIGHT steep-bank discs (radius 1.4, not the 2.4 blend) and, when filled-back lobes still leave the body small, `parkComposition` GROWS it back with probed edge basins on its open side (one body, never under guards/coaster/forecourt). And **`reclampTerrain(t, comp, rects, coasterPts?)` → `{ touched, raised, shaved, filled, expanded }`** — the AUTO-keepDry pass: re-runs the guard clamp over RUNTIME-DERIVED state (every audited footprint rect + as-built circuit points; authors' `keepDry` is a hint, not a requirement), appending discs to `comp.clampPeaks/clampBasins` IN PLACE so a terrain rebuild reproduces the healed landform. Wet samples raise to WL+0.3, bulges shave to 0.72, coaster points are ANTI-BURIAL caps (ground ≤ railY − 0.35 — footings standing in water are the RCT2 look and are never "dried"), the forecourt stays flat, one-body + water-fraction re-enforced, and corrections UNDO earlier opposite corrections near the same cell (never a raise/shave tug-of-war). `<Park>`'s settle pass calls it and `<Terrain>` rebuilds mesh/colours/heightAt in place before `validatePark` reads anything. Deterministic.
- Climate layer: `ParkClimate` (`temperate | desert | alpine | coastal`), `CLIMATES`, `climateOf(seed)` (hashed pick), `CLIMATE_SPECS` (water story, beach rays, species mix per zone, zone names, tint), `PARK_THEMES` (stripe/canvas/wood/light palettes), `tintTerrainForClimate(t, terrain.mesh, climate, basins, comp?)` (vertex-palette bias: desert tans, alpine darkens + snow-caps, fade tints keep a real beach ring; pass `comp` — as `<Terrain>` does — to ALSO paint the terrain sections: gold sand, saturated meadow, darker forest floor, banded rock above `comp.treeline`).
- `dressTerrain(t, comp, heightAt, { obstacles?, density? })` → `{ group, counts, obstacles }` — deterministic AUTO-DRESSING of the sections: Kit-tree clusters over the forest discs (climate species mix, every 2nd tree `lodDetail`-tagged), RockCluster outcrops + loose scree on the mountain flanks, dune mounds/palms/one flavour prop on the sand flank, sparse meadow loners. Keeps clear of the water, steep rock, the gate forecourt, every `comp.guardCells` (keepDry) cell, the planned coaster envelope and your obstacle discs; counts scale with area, well under the ~2500-mesh budget at size 48. `<Terrain>` mounts it automatically; imperative parks add `dressed.group` and feed `dressed.obstacles` to their scenery sampler.
- Placement helpers: `terrainLint(heightAt, size, waterLevel?)` → `{ isDry, slopeAt, flatEnough, inBounds }`; `obbOverlap(a, b)` (the 2D SAT rect test, exported for the register wrappers' round-4 queue-orientation safeguard); `planRideAccess(nodes, tailNode, dir, capacity, exit, exitDir)` + `laneLenOf(capacity)` → a GameManager-compatible `RideAccess` (queue tail ON a street node, hut at the lane head, exit on its own cell); `bermNetToGround(t, g, net, pathY, groundAt)` (earth berms + node footings so slabs never float); `plinthUnder(t, g, groundAt, x, z, w, d, topY)`; `groundRideAccess(t, g, groundAt, acc, y, resolvedExitXZ?)` (hut plinths + lane berm, returns obstacle discs).
- **`validatePark(t, { net, manager, terrain, coasters?, footprints?, group?, rebuild?, simSeconds?, gridNodeCount?, probeViolations?, pathY?, plazas?, pathWidth?, planted?, lints? })`** → `{ ok, failures, warnings, simSeconds }` — accessibility (gate routes to EVERY queue tail, AND the gate spawn stands at the terrain edge — tolerance `max(1.25, size×0.025)` u, size-relative; a gate buried inside the park FAILS); street grid (with `gridNodeCount` = the RENDERED lattice's node count, any diagonal street edge FAILS — RCT2 paths run N/S/E/W; routing-attach spurs are exempt; `<Park>` passes it automatically); terrain vibe (exactly ONE water body, flat dry apron, no footprint on a hill flank, and — round-2 safeguards — a WET-PAD guard: every audited pad/queue-lane/hut rect must stand on ground above `waterLevel + 0.05` (centre + corners sampled; a Teacups pad planted in the lake used to pass), plus `probeViolations` = `parkComposition`'s `report.violations` — non-empty means NO terrain seed fully satisfied the composition rules and is a hard terrain FAIL instead of the old console-only warn; `<Park>` passes it automatically); footprint OBB SAT sweep PLUS any residual `manager.accessAudit()` placeAccess collision (an access overlap is a FAIL, never a console-only warn); coaster legality (a `compileTrackPieces` `fatal` circuit — pass it via `coasters[].fatal` — is ONE hard failure; otherwise `checkCoasterDesign` + IN-BOUNDS: every sampled track point within ±size/2 + 0.4 + `validateSpline` + crash-free: worst lateral G under 1.5 g × 0.85; the re-check saturates `coasters[].bank` at the type's bankLimit via `coasterBankCap` — same clamp the kit builds with); a GameManager smoke run over `max(SIM_SMOKE_SECONDS, 2.5·maxRideDuration + 20)` sim-s so long rides can complete a cycle (≥1 ride cycle, no stuck walkers, no stalled queue — the stall threshold scales too; a registered `rideDuration > 14` draws a soft "acceptance sims need ≤ ~12 s rides" warning); mesh budget — AREA-SCALED, ~2500 per 16² of plot (~22 500 at the default 48); exceeding it is a `console.warn`, NOT a hard fail (LOD + fog keep big parks drawable, but warnings are fatal by the recipe-book policy) — + double-build determinism hash; and the TRACK-CORRIDOR sweep (round-4, failure kind `corridor`): a 1.6-u-wide corridor of segment OBBs along every registered circuit's sampled polyline must be CLEAR of stall footprints (`manager.stalls()`), OTHER rides' pads/huts/lanes (the ride's OWN access is exempt — pass `coasters[].name`, the registered ride name; `<Coaster>`/`<TrackRide>` do) and street edges at grade — an obstacle only fails when the track passes within 2.2 u of its level (`pathY` = the slab walking level for the street check; default terrain height), so flying OVER on a high leg stays clean while grade crossings and roof-skims FAIL naming the offender. The smoke run advances the sim clock — shift your updater time by the report's `simSeconds` afterwards, as `<Park>` and the example do. **ROUND-6 ADDITIONS.** (1) `lints` — build-time auto-fix events (`<Park>` passes `ParkContext.reportLint` records): every one is echoed in the new `report.warnings: { kind, detail, fatal }[]` and the `fatal` ones become hard `'autofix'` failures, because rules/park-generation.md §0 calls a rescued design fatal — a queueDir auto-flip, a hoisted causeway, a corridor shift or a relocated exit hut used to leave `ok: true`. An EMPTY `warnings` array is the only clean result. (2) `'bounds'` — every audited footprint's SWEPT corners and every GameManager-DERIVED queue-tail/exit attach node (`net.nodes[accessPoints().rides[].queueNode]`) must sit inside ±size/2; a capacity-10 rig against the fence used to put queue tails a metre off the plot and pass. (3) `'scenery'` — `planted` (`{ label, x, z, r }`, from `<Scenery>`/`<Placed>`) is audited for DRYNESS (waterLevel + 0.05 under the footprint ring) and for standing inside a rendered path slab (`pathWidth`, default 1.1; `plazas` are exempt dressing; the blocking radius caps at 0.6 u so a walk-in gazebo abutting a path is legal). (4) the track-corridor sweep gains a STRICT re-audit: beside the 1.6-u corridor / 2.2-u ground clearance, every rect is re-swept 2.2 u wide against the obstacle's OWN ROOF (hut 1.5, stall canopy 2.1, lane 0.45, pad 0.6, + 0.3 clearance) — a relocated hut 0.96 u from the rails with the track 0.59 u off the ground used to pass.

Fully deterministic — hashed-sine randomness only; the same seed always composes identically.
