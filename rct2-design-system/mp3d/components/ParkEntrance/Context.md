# ParkEntrance

The RCT2 PARK entrance gate: two square stone/masonry towers (concrete texture + bump, corner quoins, cornice) with pyramidal slate roofs and finials, flanking a wide archway over the path. A deep stone beam spans the opening with blocky corner haunches, carrying a white marquee sign band on BOTH faces — dark trim stripes plus 12 dark geometric bar strokes read as the park name, no text textures. Each tower front has a recessed ticket-booth window with a stone sill, lintel AND jambs (the dark pane sits 4 mm proud inside the proud surround — it reads inset, never extruded), a night-gated warm lantern (real PointLight, gated via `nightKOf` on the lamp mesh's `onBeforeRender` — off by day, glowing at night) and wrought-iron fence stubs with ball-finial posts running outward — first post + both rails embedded into the shaft, no daylight gap at the tower face. A paved threshold slab carries the path through the arch. Contact audit (2026-07): roof pyramids overhang the cornice by 0.06 and embed 0.01 (no coplanar cap), the marquee band ends 0.08 INSIDE the tower inner corners (it must never reach the tower fronts at z ±0.36 — a coplanar board z-fights there), and the haunches embed 0.01 into the tower inner faces.

`buildParkEntrance(t, { width? })` → `{ group, spawnPoint, archway }` — EXACT contract the GameManager codes against: the group faces local +z with the park OUTSIDE at +z; `spawnPoint` (`[0, 0, ~0.91]`) is just OUTSIDE the arch where new guests appear, and `archway` (`[0, 0, 0]`) is under the arch at ground level. `width` is the clear arch opening in x (default 1.6 — two 0.5-scale peeps abreast; beam underside at y ≈ 1.26 clears them easily). Deterministic geometry, no update fn needed.

Original three.js composition on the shared Stage; proportions and the twin-tower + marquee-arch look referenced from the canonical RCT2 park entrance, realistic palette only.
