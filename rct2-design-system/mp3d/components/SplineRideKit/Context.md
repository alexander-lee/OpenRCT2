# SplineRideKit

Generalises SplineCoaster's control-point workflow into a spline RIDE kit: one closed centripetal Catmull-Rom spline (parallel-transport frames, curvature banking, loop-closure twist correction — all imported from SplineCoaster) composes five ride profiles. `'coaster'` delegates the mesh to `buildSplineCoaster` and layers RCT2 construction rules + crash physics on top; `'flume'` sweeps a brown wooden U-channel trough with a natural blue-grey water strip (0x447588 — matches WaterTile's desaturated lake palette; banking capped at ~0.25); `'rapids'` is the rocky stone-channel variant (strip 0x447588; water strip height via `opts.waterY`); `'bobsled'` an icy pale-blue half-pipe chute (hard banking, 1.5 g derail guard); `'gokart'` a flat grey asphalt ribbon with alternating red/white kerb blocks along both edges (bank ≈ 0, gentle-slope cap, near-constant kart pace). All profiles share ground-aware supports (`groundAt`) and lift-hill detection with a chain/belt strip.

Compatibility shim (round-2 safeguard): `rideColourPreset` (+ the `RideColourScheme` type) is RE-EXPORTED here because generated parks keep importing it from SplineRideKit beside `compileTrackPieces` and one wrong-source import used to fail the whole bundle. It LIVES in ColorKit — import it from there.

`buildRideSpline(t, controlPoints, opts?)` → `{ group, curve, frameAt(u), run(vehicles, runOpts?), valid, design?, setBrakesFailure(v), crashed(), forceCrash(), resetCrash() }`. Opts: `profile`, `type` ('wooden'|'steel'|'inverted'), `bank`, `supportEvery`, `groundAt`, `wood`, `waterY`.

**Bank saturation (round-3 safeguard):** `coasterBankCap(type, requested?)` — the curvature-banking cap a coaster may build with: `min(requested ?? 0.7, TYPE_RULES[type].maxBank)` (wooden 25°, steel/inverted 55°). The 0.7 rad kit default used to auto-bank wooden layouts to 36-40°, failing `bankLimit` at documented archetype params whenever no `bank` was passed. `compileTrackPieces` (design frames), `buildRideSpline`'s coaster profile (geometry AND check frames), `<Coaster>` (registered bank) and `validatePark`'s coaster re-check all clamp through it, so they always agree; the design check's ~5° TOL absorbs the residual roll parallel transport adds on top of the cap. `run` paces +z-facing vehicle groups by arc length: energy-paced for coaster/bobsled, near-constant drift for flume/rapids, constant throttle for gokart; runOpts adds `laneOffset` (sideways shift along frame.side — kart lanes) and `speedScale` beside speed/spacing/wheelOffset.

## Construction rules (RCT2)

`checkCoasterDesign(t, curve, { type?, frames? })` → `{ ok, violations: { kind, at, detail, warning? }[] }` — runs automatically for the coaster profile, one console.warn per violation (same pattern as `validateSpline`). Per-type track whitelists from OpenRCT2, `ride/rtd/coaster/WoodenRollerCoaster.h:26,85`: **wooden** slopes ≤ 60°, banking ≤ ~25° with gradual transitions, NO inversions/corkscrews; **steel** slopes ≤ 90°, bank ≤ ~55°, inversions allowed; **inverted** like steel but no drops steeper than ~75°. Violation kinds: `slope`, `pitchRate` (|Δpitch|/Δarc > ~0.55 rad/unit — RCT2 track climbs in FIXED slope steps, flat/25°/60°, joined by dedicated one-tile TRANSITION pieces like flat-to-25 and 25-to-60, never arbitrary kinks; one 25° step per tile-ish arc length is the ceiling), `bankRate` (|Δroll|/Δarc > ~0.9 rad/unit — RCT2 banks only through transition pieces), `bankLimit`, `inversion` (up-vector flip), `noStation` (no flat straight ≥ 2.2 units in the lowest quarter — an incomplete circuit cannot open, `Ride.cpp:5648`), plus stat-gate WARNINGS `shortDrop` (first drop right after the lift under ~0.9 units) and `shortLength` (`RideRatings.cpp:1318,2215`) — they do not fail `checkCoasterDesign` itself, but `<Coaster>` reports both to the park as lints and **`validatePark` promotes `coaster:shortDrop` to a hard `'autofix'` FAILURE** (round-6, rules/park-generation.md §0): a drop under the 0.9-u gate halves all three ratings, so design the lift at ≥ 1.0. `validateSpline(t, curve, opts?)` → `{ ok, worst, at }` remains the clearance guard (parameter-distant samples must keep > 0.9 units).

## Track-piece compiler

`compileTrackPieces(pieces, opts?)` → `{ points, report }` — RCT2-style piece authoring for every spline ride. `pieces` is an array of names or `{ type, length?, height?, radius?, angle? }` descriptors; vocabulary (every piece starts and ends LEVEL): `station` (flat boarding straight — put it FIRST), `flat`/`straight`, `lift`/`drop` (rampPoints-eased, pitch-legal by construction; drop height defaults to "back to station level"), `hill` (camelback), `turnL`/`turnR` (angle default 90°, radius 1.5), `helixL`/`helixR` (angle 360°, eased height), `corkscrewL`/`corkscrewR` (a genuinely inverting planar loop leaned ~20°, R:advance 1:4 — STEEL coasters only; wooden warns per TRACK_WHITELIST semantics, water/monorail profiles substitute an sbend, since PT holonomy of a NON-planar barrel would twist every other frame), `sbend`. Opts: `profile` ('coaster' default, plus flume/rapids/bobsled/gokart/'monorail'), `type` (gates corkscrews), `start` (default `[0, 0.55, 0]`), `heading` (yaw, 0 = +z), `bank` (forwarded to the design frames; profile-matched defaults otherwise — for the coaster profile it SATURATES at the type's bankLimit via `coasterBankCap`, see below), `bounds` (the park's terrain size — or `{ half, margin? }` — every compiled point must stay within ±half + 0.3; **closure-synthesized pieces COUNT toward the bounds**, so land the last authored piece ~0.3 u SHORT of the start — an overshooting leg makes the Dubins closure wrap around outside the park). CLOSURE: the cursor walk ends wherever the pieces leave it, then the compiler closes the circuit legally — an eased ramp back to station height along the current heading, then the shortest Dubins arc–straight–arc (radius 1.5; coasters 2.2) onto the start pose — coasters land through a synthesized 1.2-u straight BRAKE TAIL in front of the station (round-4 safeguard: a closing arc welded directly onto the station concentrated the wrap-point curvature and validatePark's crash replay read a lateral-G spike on the fastest, least-banked track); `report.closure.synthesized` lists what it added and it warns when the return leg is suspiciously long. When the AUTHORED pieces land on the station themselves, the compiler warns "the circuit lands on the station from a curve" whenever the final kept point sits > 0.3 u off the station axis — end layouts with a straight brake tail >= ~1.2 u landing ~0.3 u short (the re-verified rules §4 archetypes do). `report` = `{ ok, design (checkCoasterDesign — enforced for the coaster profile, informational otherwise), valid (validateSpline), closure, warnings, fatal?, fatalReason? }` — both validators ALWAYS run. **FATAL guard (round-1 safeguard):** `report.fatal` is set when the checks fail, when the synthesized closure exceeds 40% of the authored arc length (the layout didn't come back to the station), or when any point leaves `opts.bounds` — a fatal circuit must NOT be registered as a working ride: `<Coaster>`/`<TrackRide>` render it as a translucent red "invalid" track, skip the GameManager registration, console.error the fix (end the layout FACING the station; keep pieces inside the park) and hand the circuit to validatePark, which fails hard on it. Deterministic and pure. checkCoasterDesign gained inversion-element windows: for types whose piece table has inversions, samples within ~2.8 arc-units of an up-vector flip are exempt from the slope/pitch/bank sweeps (fixed-geometry elements, like RCT2's corkscrew TrackElemTypes); non-inversion types still fail `inversion`. React layer: Park exports the JSX piece components (`<Station/> <Lift/> <TurnR/> …`), `collectTrackPieces`, `<TrackRide profile>` and piece support on `<Coaster>`; LogFlume/RiverRapids/Monorail/Bobsleigh accept `pieces`/piece children (SETUP §5.1). `buildMiniLog`/`buildMiniSled` are exported as TrackRide's default flume/bobsled vehicles; `ribbon`/`railTube` are exported sweep helpers (Monorail's loop beam uses them).

`rampPoints(from, dir, { run, rise, steps? })` → `[x, y, z][]` — composes control points shaped like an RCT2-legal ramp, mirroring the game's piece grammar: flat lead-in → eased transition (quarter-sine gradient, the flat-to-25 piece) → constant-gradient middle capped at 60° (the steepest slope piece) → eased top transition → flat run-out. `dir` is the horizontal heading `[dx, dz]` (normalised internally), `rise` may be negative for a drop, and the run is auto-extended whenever the requested rise would break the 60° cap or the pitchRate ceiling — so ramps composed with it pass `slope` and `pitchRate` by construction (verified: a loop of two rampPoints ramps checks clean at 0.34 rad/unit max). `steps` defaults to about one control point per 0.8-unit "tile". Pure, deterministic, no THREE needed — splice the points into any buildRideSpline / computeSplineFrames layout.

## Ride ratings (RCT2) — `rateCoaster`

`rateCoaster(points, opts?)` → the RCT2 stat block for a compiled circuit (control points from `compileTrackPieces`, or any closed list — no THREE argument, same as `compileTrackPieces`):

```ts
const { points } = compileTrackPieces(PIECES, { type: 'wooden', start, heading, bounds: 48 });
const r = rateCoaster(points, { type: 'wooden', bank: 0.42, cars: 3 });
// r.excitement 0.59  r.intensity 0.78  r.nausea 0.37  r.ratingBand 'gentle'
```

Returns `{ excitement, intensity, nausea, maxSpeed, avgSpeed, maxPosVertG, maxNegVertG, maxLatG, highestDrop, totalDrop, dropCount, airtimeSeconds, inversions, length, duration, ratingBand, nauseaBand, nauseaExtreme, type, turns }`. Opts: `type` (which RTD ratings table — default `'wooden'`), `bank` (**pass what you BUILT with** so the forces match the ride — it is re-saturated through `coasterBankCap`), `frames` (skip the resample), `cars` (BonusTrainLength, default 3), `sceneryScore` (BonusScenery 0-40, default 0), `tileUnits` (world units per RCT2 tile, default 1).

**ONE physics model.** The measurement pass is `replayCoasterForces(t, points, opts?)` — the energy-paced replay factored OUT of `validatePark`'s crash gate (which now calls it) so the gate and the ratings can never drift apart. It returns `{ frames, samples, worstLatAccel, worstLatAt, maxY, onLift, duration }` with one sample per frame: `{ u, y, v, kH, roll, latAccel, vertG }`. Speed is the runner's `√(2·(g·(maxY+0.55) − g·y))` (vMin 1.4, lift 1.5, brake run 1.6); `latAccel` is the guard's bank-discounted `v²·κ_h·(1 − min(1, |roll|/0.6))` (`Vehicle.TrackMotion.cpp:58-122`) — so **`maxLatG` IS the number validatePark tests against its 1.27 g margin** (1.5 g × 0.85); `vertG` reads the same state along the car's up axis (gravity term + centripetal — the shape of `Vehicle::GetGForces`, `Vehicle.cpp:1173-1199`).

Then RideRatings.cpp's chain runs over the measurements (`RideRatings.cpp:876-1051`): base ratings + BonusLength/TrainLength/MaxSpeed/AverageSpeed/Duration/GForces/Turns/Drops, the `Requirement*` **halvings** (drop height, max speed 22.5 mph, negative Gs, length, drop count — relaxed on inverting layouts per `RelaxRequirementsIfInversions`), the air-time adjustment (`:1293-1310`), `PenaltyLateralGs` (`:2215-2245`) and the universal intensity penalty (`:1318-1330`). Tables are ported verbatim from `ride/rtd/coaster/WoodenRollerCoaster.h:63-92` (`wooden`), `LoopingRollerCoaster.h:64-91` (`steel`) and `InvertedRollerCoaster.h:65-91` (`inverted`). `ratingBand`/`nauseaBand` are RCT2's own rating words (`openrct2-ui/windows/Ride.cpp:501-509,5628-5632`, `index = rating >> 8`) mapped onto five: gentle / moderate / thrilling / intense / extreme. `nauseaExtreme` = nausea ≥ 8.00, past the AVERAGE guest's tolerance (`Guest.cpp:193-198`).

**Unit mapping.** The kit authors its pieces roughly ONE PER RCT2 TILE (straight 1.3, turn radius 1.5, station 2.6 world units against RCT2's 1-3-tile equivalents), so **one world unit is read as one RCT2 tile** (`tileUnits` overrides). Everything else follows from RCT2's own arithmetic: 6.8175 length units per tile ((32 × 0x368A) >> 16 — `Vehicle.cpp:497`, `UnitConversion.cpp:74`), 9.3495 mph per tile/second (`Vehicle.cpp:497` vs `UnitConversion.cpp:62`), 4 height steps per tile (`kCoordsZStep` 8 in a 32-coord tile), 0.8 s per duration unit. G-forces are dimensionless and pass through 1:1 — the replay is already calibrated to RCT2's own g thresholds.

**Deliberately SIMPLIFIED vs RideRatings.cpp:** no BonusSheltered / BonusProximity (DS circuits are open-air, and there is no tile map to score against — `sceneryScore` covers BonusScenery by hand), no BonusSynchronisation / BonusReversedTrains / helix / water-splash special elements, no rideEntry excitement/intensity/nausea multipliers (they live on the vehicle OBJECT, which the DS has no analogue for), each turn lands in exactly ONE of RCT2's flat/banked/sloped buckets (RCT2 can flag a piece both banked and sloped), and — exactly as `checkCoasterDesign` exempts them from its slope/bank sweeps — **inversion-element windows** (within ~2.8 arc-units of an up-vector flip) are excluded from the vertical-G peak, the air-time tally and the drop scan: the kit's compact planar loop (R ≈ 0.6 u) would read tens of g where RCT2 uses a fixed TrackElemType with a pre-baked `verticalFactor` table. RCT2 pays for inversions through `getInversionsRatings` instead, which is what runs.

`<Coaster>` and `<TrackRide profile="coaster">` measure their as-built circuit automatically and pass the triple to `registerRide({ ratings })`; read it back with `handle.ratings()` (null when nobody measured one) — `RideViewer`'s `ride` prop accepts the optional `ratings()` accessor so the window can show the real Excitement/Intensity/Nausea lines. `mgr.rides()` rows also carry `intensity` (the sim's 1-10 guest gate) and `ratings`.

## Crash conditions (RCT2)

> **Coasters are CRASH-FREE BY DEFAULT.** A normally authored, rule-legal layout never crashes on its own: crashes happen ONLY when a user explicitly forces one (`forceCrash()`), explicitly breaks the brakes (`setBrakesFailure(true)`), or deliberately authors a dangerous layout past the physics guards (e.g. an unbanked hairpin over 1.5 g lateral, like the clearly-labelled derail demo). Every shipped preview/demo layout has been numerically audited against the guards — worst effective lateral: MINI_COASTER 0.26 g, MINI_BOB 0.13 g, Bobsleigh 0.55 g, all far under the 1.5 g derail threshold; only the derail demo exceeds it (7.6 g, by design).

Guarded profiles (coaster, bobsled — cars with no upstop wheels): the runner derails the train when effective lateral force `v²·κ_h·(1 − min(1, |roll|/0.6))` exceeds **1.5 g** on under-banked track (`Vehicle.TrackMotion.cpp:58-122`, which also derails past vertical −0.8 g). `setBrakesFailure(true)` reproduces the station-brakes breakdown (`Vehicle.Station.cpp:1385-1418,1640`): the train skips the brake run and on completing its lap rams the parked phantom train at the station — closing speed far above the 14 mph bump threshold (`Vehicle.Collision.cpp:52`), so it always crashes. `forceCrash()` crashes immediately; `crashed()` reports state; `resetCrash()` clears the wreck and relaunches.

`crashTrain(t, parentGroup, cars, frameVel, { groundAt? })` → `{ update(time), done(), dispose() }` plays the RCT2 crash sequence (`Vehicle.Crash.cpp:169-585`): each car detaches ballistically ∝ velocity with hashed jitter, tumbles under gravity, and on ground impact spawns 2 expanding emissive fireballs (orange 0xff9a3c → fading), 6 scattering debris boxes, 3 flame cones burning ~2.4 s then smouldering embers + rising smoke, and a charred wreck — plus ParticleKit layers per car (~136 particles): a one-shot additive SPARK burst at the impact point, a rising grey SMOKE PLUME whose emission rate tracks the burn-down, and an additive fire shimmer over the flame cones; all disposed with the wreck. Riders are removed with the car. Fully deterministic — jitter hashed by car index, t0 taken from the first `update` call (no Math.random / Date.now).

Preview shows four minis: steel coaster, flume, bobsled — all rule-legal (the coaster minis follow the ramp grammar: plateau crests, descents spread over the loop, max pitch rate ≤ 0.47 rad/unit) — and a DERAIL DEMO (unbanked hairpin at ~7.6 g effective lateral, past the 1.5 g no-upstop threshold) whose train launches, derails at the hairpin, explodes and resets ~6 s later; its slopes are pitch-legal — it demos lateral G, not slope. Coaster trains use CoasterCar variants in RCT2 order: `buildCoasterCar(t, 'front')` leads (nose + headlight), `'end'` trails (tail fairing + red tail-light gated by `gateCarLights`). Palette is realistic (wood browns, ice blue-white, steel greys, asphalt grey 0x4a4d52).
